# 🦟 MOSQUITO MISSION: ZERO BITE
### Community Service Project (CSP) • Interactive Casual Mini-Game Collection

> **“Play. Learn. Protect.”**  
> A cheerful, visual, child-friendly mini-game collection built for our Community Service Project (CSP). The game accompanies our physical natural mosquito-repellent candle: **“Light Against Bite”**.

---

## 🌟 Visual Mini-Game Philosophy: SHOW → PLAY → LEARN → WIN/LOSE

Every game is designed to be instantly understood just by looking at the screen—no reading long instructions or manuals!

1. **🍪 Game 1: BISCUIT BATTLE** (*"Save the Biscuit!"*)
   - A biscuit hangs above a hot cup of tea and slowly sinks as time passes!
   - Answer before it dips: **Correct** yanks it up (*SAFE & CRUNCHY! 😋*), **Too Slow / Wrong** dunks it (*SPLASH! 💦 SOGGY BISCUIT!*).
2. **🦟 Game 2: DISEASE DETECTIVE** (*"Trap the Mosquito!"*)
   - An animated mosquito buzzes across the screen. Answering correctly commands a visible hand to swoop in: **✋ → 🦟 → TRAP! 🫙**. Wrong answers make the hand miss and the mosquito dodge away!
3. **🪣 Game 3: MOSQUITO WATER DROP** (*"Stop the Breeding!"*)
   - A cartoon water bucket slowly fills with rising water and floating mosquito eggs! Answer correctly to drain the water (**💧⬇️**). If it reaches the top, eggs hatch into mosquitoes (**🦟🦟🦟**)!
4. **🌀 Game 4: MOSQUITO VS FAN** (*"Keep the Mosquito Away!"*)
   - Fan on the left, flying mosquito in the center, and a person on the right. Correct answers spin the fan into overdrive (**🌀🌀🌀💨💨💨**) blowing the mosquito backwards! Wrong answers slow the fan.
5. **🛏️ Game 5: MOSQUITO & NET** (*"Protect the Sleeper!"*)
   - A cartoon friend sleeps in bed while a mosquito approaches. Correct answers lower the protective bed net (**🛏️😴⬇️🕸️**). Mosquito bonks into the sealed net and flies away!
6. **🚫 Game 6: MYTH OR FACT** (*"Zap the Mosquito Lies!"*)
   - A cartoon mosquito holds up funny statement signs. Tap **❌ MYTH** or **✅ FACT**! Correct answers shock the mosquito with electric eyes (**⚡😵**); wrong answers make it laugh (**😂**)!
7. **🌱 Game 7: ECO HERO** (*"Pick the Green Choice!"*)
   - Choose responsible green vector options over toxic dumping. The garden immediately blooms with flowers (**🌸**), butterflies (**🦋**), and clean water!
8. **🏠 Game 8: HOUSE ESCAPE** (*"Trap the Mosquito in the House!"*)
   - A mosquito buzzes inside a house with 4 open exits (front window, front door, bedroom window, back door). Each correct answer locks an exit (**🔒**). Seal all 4 to trap the mosquito in a jar (**🫙✨**)!

---

## 📚 96+ Question Educational Bank
- **12+ unique, randomized questions** per game across all 8 public health topics.
- Shuffled options per round so the correct answer is never predictably in the same spot.
- Covers daytime biting of Aedes (Dengue, Chikungunya, Zika), night biting of Anopheles (Malaria), warning signs, breeding eradication, wind dynamics, bed nets, myths, and eco-safety.

- ☁️ Clean, bright, cheerful palette (soft greens, sky blues, warm yellows, white rounded cards).
